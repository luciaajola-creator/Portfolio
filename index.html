AI and LLM · Automation

AI Pipeline Confidence Agent

Problem: No measure of how reliable the open pipeline was. Hygiene reviews were manual.

Result: A read-only agent runs each weekday. It scores completeness, activity, and value-weighted confidence, then posts a team summary and per-owner messages to Slack. Baseline: 55% of open opportunities pass all checks, 85% field completeness, 68% with logged activity. Re-measurement scheduled at 30 days.

Key decision and stack
  • During validation the LLM miscounted records and assigned opportunities to the wrong owners. I moved counting and attribution into deterministic code and kept the LLM for drafting only.
  • Every figure was checked against live Salesforce queries before sending.
  • Slack write-back bot: unique-match check, value validation, user allowlist, dry-run by default.
  • Stack: n8n, Salesforce SOQL, LLM API, Slack API.
Automation · Salesforce

Meeting to Opportunity (WF1)

Problem: Booked meetings did not reliably create opportunities, and recreating deleted records caused duplicate loops.

Result: Fuzzy owner matching, a duplicate guard, idempotency keys, and a fallback owner alert. The deletion-and-recreation loop is fixed.

Details
  • Idempotency check now also finds soft-deleted records in the recycle bin.
  • Alerts instead of creating a second opportunity when an active one exists.
Automation · Routing

Real-Time Inbound Lead Routing

Problem: Inbound leads were routed by hand, with delays and inconsistent ownership.

Result: Polling trigger, normalization, filtering, regional reassignment, and team alerts. Debugging found a silent-drop branch and a PATCH response that overwrote lead data; both are fixed.

AI and LLM · Salesforce

Gong to Salesforce Discovery Intelligence

Problem: Call insights never reached CRM fields, so deal reviews relied on memory.

Result: An LLM extracts about 28 MEDDPICC fields into the opportunity and drafts an AE follow-up. A parallel branch writes competitive data to a reportable child object, tested end-to-end on a live call without touching the production update.

RevOps · Data quality

Salesforce Data Quality and Structural Cleanup

Problem: Years of fields, flows, and automations caused errors, with no usable sandbox.

Result: 89 unused fields removed across five objects in two production deployments. The 39 blocked fields each have a documented blocker. Three reports from one day traced to one dirty-value bug, fixed at the source with a restricted picklist.

More
  • Cloned, edited, tested, and activated a process version live in production, then retired the old versions that blocked field deletion.
  • About 10,000 orphaned tasks from a scheduled managed-package job: job stopped, full ID snapshot taken, 88% deleted in batches. The rest were documented rather than backfilled.
RevOps · HubSpot

Inbound Scoring and Qualification

Problem: Six weeks with zero MQLs, traced to a misrouted workflow property.

Result: Diagnosed, recalibrated, and reactivated the scoring model. Added automatic disqualification with a stored reason. Auto-MQL infrastructure built with a dry-run list and held until calibration was validated.

Automated GTM Reporting

Problem: Weekly and monthly pipeline decks took hours of manual work.

Result: Workflows pull Salesforce data and generate decks in Google Drive with template folders, automatic naming, and verification before release.
